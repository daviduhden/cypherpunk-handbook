# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone participating in Cypherpunk Handbook discussions and contributions.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive when discussing ideas or reviewing work.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to feedback and correction.
- Critique proposals and code without attacking people.
- Respect differing backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Keep project discussions focused and considerate of other participants' time.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include harassment, hateful or discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, publishing another person's private information without consent, and sustained disruptive behavior. This also includes abusive conduct in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Reporting and response

For non-sensitive concerns, contact the maintainers through a GitHub issue or pull request. For a private or sensitive report, use the email address published on [contact.html](contact.html). Please include enough context to identify the relevant interaction; do not post private information publicly.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, keep them confidential to the extent practical, and take action proportionate to the circumstances. Responses may include a warning, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces. Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is representing the project in a public community.
